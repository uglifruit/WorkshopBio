BioMimicry — sample uploader

Drop your own recordings onto the card over USB. No rebuild, no toolchain. Chrome or Edge (WebMIDI).

First, hold the card's momentary switch down for two seconds — the LEDs fill left to right, and all six stay lit once it has handed itself over to USB. The card does not appear to your computer until you do: it runs no USB at all while it is playing, which is what keeps the audio path clear. Then press Connect. To go back to playing, hold the switch again or press Back to playing — either restarts the card.

Card storage

Timing — worst case per sample

Budget is 2604 cycles (20.83 µs at 125 MHz). Anything over that means the DMA interrupt for the next sample arrives while this one is still being processed.

These numbers are from before you held the switch. The ecosystem stops when the card hands itself over to USB, so nothing is being measured now — the peaks are the ones it reached while you were playing, and they survive until this button reads them. So: play the mode you want to test, then hold, connect, and click.

Each row is its own worst sample, so they do not add up. The physics run on one sample in 32 and the voices run on all of them, so Engine's peak and Voices' peak are almost never the same sample — their sum routinely exceeds Total, which is the worst single sample and the only figure the budget applies to. Compare a row against itself between builds, never against the others.

Any WAV works — any sample rate, mono or stereo. The browser converts to the card's format (8-bit mono 48 kHz), trims silence and matches loudness across everything you load, exactly as tools/importwav.py does. Empty slots keep whatever is already baked into the firmware, so you can replace one ecosystem and leave the rest alone.

The card keeps playing while it receives, then stops for the final write and restarts into your new samples.

Uploads add to what is already on the card — send one sample, listen to it, then send another and you end up with both. To clear a mode, use its own Revert … to built-in button; to clear everything and reclaim the space, use Revert to built-in above. Space is consumed by every upload, including replacements of the same slot.
